Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
"The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting" — one razor-sharp line, at
most 200 characters, that excuses you from a pointless work meeting or
Zoom call. An AI judge pairs excuses head to head and keeps the sharper,
more relatable laugh.

## The entry

> Sorry, I can't join — my laptop read the invite, started a two-hour
> update, and honestly, I respect its judgment.

113 characters. The joke lands on a twist everyone who has ever worked
in an office recognises: the forced two-hour update is the one
appointment nobody can argue with — and here the laptop itself is
dodging the meeting, so the owner simply agrees with it.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the line as a single constant and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (or to the path in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` when set), which
is what the make.fun program/v1 harness grades: UTF-8 text, 1 to 200
characters, not only whitespace. No network, no secrets, no files
outside this repository.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program
- `output/entry.txt` — the entry, generated by running `main.py`
